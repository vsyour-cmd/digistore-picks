Why we publish churn at all

A directory that only shows what exists today hides the single most informative signal: how fast this market turns over. New listings have no track record (their stats are missing for a reason); disappeared listings had buyers yesterday. If you use this site to pick offers, the churn rate is your prior for how much verification any single listing deserves — and our price tracker keeps the full movement history.
