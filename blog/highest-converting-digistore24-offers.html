Why high conversion alone is not a buying signal

Three reasons to read the cancel column before the conversion column:

  • Aggressive funnels convert and refund. A 40% cart conversion with a 25% cancel rate nets far less than it looks — and refund-heavy buyers hurt the promoter's reputation too.
  • Traffic quality is the hidden variable. These percentages describe the vendor's existing traffic mix. Your audience may convert very differently.
  • Price context matters. A $27 offer converting at 35% and a $2,000 coaching program converting at 35% are entirely different businesses — always check the price column.
