Opening copy

The Bald Sparrow is a bittersweet fable about a small sparrow born without feathers on his head, who endures ridicule and rejection from the other birds. Despite the pain of being mocked and excluded, he eventually finds someone who values him for who he is rather than how he looks. This touching tale imparts a timeless lesson: true worth lies in kindness and character, not in outward appearances.
This emotionally rich tale is complemented by Zhann , the heartfelt story of a noble dog who is devoted to his kind owner but becomes tempted by the wild, carefree life of a stray. Drawn to a beautiful but unfaithful dog, he abandons his home—only to face heartbreak and a brutal attack. Left wounded and alone, he realizes too late the value of true love and loyalty. But when his owner finds him and nurses him back to health, Zhann is given a second chance—one he vows to cherish forever.
Together, the two tales offer powerful messages of humility, devotion, and personal growth.
