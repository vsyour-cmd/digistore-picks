Opening copy

Island of Morality is a symbolic and reflective fiction book about the virtues that escape a world losing its moral center and arrive on a mysterious island shaped by fog, hunger, silence, and hidden tests.
Through Truth, Generosity, Modesty, Loyalty, Mercy, Justice, Hope, and the memory of Patience, the story follows human values under pressure. Each virtue faces fear, weakness, need, and the cost of remaining true when survival itself becomes difficult.
This is not a direct moral lecture. It is a symbolic journey about conscience, community, survival, and the fragile line between protecting what is good and becoming what one once feared.
