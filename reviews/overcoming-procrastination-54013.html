Opening copy

You don’t struggle with getting things done because you’re lazy, you struggle because distractions, self-doubt, and frustrating tasks keep pulling you off course. You start your day with big plans, only to scroll, postpone, or push things aside, and by evening, guilt and frustration leave you feeling stuck. The cycle repeats, leaving your goals farther away and your confidence chipped away, making every “tomorrow” feel like a promise you can’t keep.
